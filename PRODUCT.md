## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Aaron Ouyang's personal website collects his work in software, videos, and 3D design, with an about page and a useful OLED wallpaper checker.

## Users

Visitors exploring Aaron's work and people checking their wallpapers. Audience inferred from the existing pages; no narrower recruitment or commercial target has been specified.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing Next.js site; keep its routes, external destinations, resume, and contact links working.
- Home redirects to About. Projects, Videos, 3D, and OLED are separate pages.
- OLED analysis stays entirely on the visitor's device; upload, paste, and drag-and-drop must remain functional.
- Keep the user's project descriptions verbatim, including Noctia's existing description.
- No OLED link in the header; discover it through Projects.
- This is a local aesthetic experiment, not a deployment or commit request.

## Brand Commitments

Aaron requested a noticeable redesign that keeps the dark, personal feel. Preserve the informal first-person voice and existing factual content. This is Aaron's site, not Ryan's.

## Evidence on Hand

The current app routes contain the approved copy and links. Videos has four real YouTube videos and fetched titles/thumbnails. The professional 3D portfolio and ArtStation are external; no local gallery imagery has been supplied. Resume is public/AaronOuyang_Resume.pdf.

## Product Principles

- Let the person's work and writing carry the identity.
- Keep navigation and useful tools straightforward.
- Preserve real content; do not invent biography, claims, or portfolio imagery.
